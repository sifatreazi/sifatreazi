# Sifat  
PhD, Urban & Environmental Planning & Policy  
University of California, Irvine  

As a researcher and policy analyst, I employ an interdisciplinary lens across informatics, urban planning, and public policy to evaluate issues around digital privacy, refugee management, and migration. In an effort to center the refugee narratives, her work strives to be participatory, community-based and oriented to ‘study up’ in an effort to effect policy development in collaboration with the communities they impact.
